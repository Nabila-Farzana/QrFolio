QR Profile Generator

Make a QR code that opens your contact profile with your attachments.

1 Details

Write a name, and give at least one contact: a phone number, an email address, or a website.

2 Attachments

Add your files, for example a portfolio, a price list, a certificate, or photos. Choose them from your Google Drive or Dropbox, or paste a link. The files stay in your own account. This site does not store them.

Use a public link only. Every person who scans the QR code must open the file with no login. Test each link in a private browser window.
